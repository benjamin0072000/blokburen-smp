## Sfeerillustratie

`public/images/buurt.webp` is één AI-gegenereerde fictieve Minecraft-buurt, gemaakt met de ingebouwde image_gen-tool. Dit beeld is geen echte serverscreenshot. Het bijschrift maakt dat zichtbaar. Geoptimaliseerd naar 1400 pixels breed met WebP quality 82.

Gebruikte prompt:

> Wide 3:2 website atmosphere illustration for the right side of a warm cream and orange homepage. A fictional Minecraft voxel neighborhood in afternoon sunshine, a cozy small survival settlement with four block-built houses: white walls, orange terracotta roofs on three houses and a single aqua roof. Green block trees, a winding path, a small crop field, and a clear narrow stream weave through the settlement. Polished premium game render, unmistakable authentic Minecraft block geometry and pixel-material textures, crisp square edges, orthographic gently isometric camera. Mature, welcoming, carefully composed. The village and landscape fill the canvas, not a floating miniature island, no border. Houses comfortably spaced among terrain, with visible paths and stream. Warm cream sky. Warm afternoon sunlight with soft ambient shadows, peaceful, lived-in and inviting. Cream, terracotta orange, leafy green, one aqua roof, restrained blue water. Exactly one final image. This is an explicitly fictional atmosphere illustration, not an actual server screenshot. No text, no interface, no logo, no watermark, no characters, no rounded toy-style shapes.
